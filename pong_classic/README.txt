PONG - Classic Arcade

Run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep pong.html and the assets folder together.
3. Open pong.html in a modern browser.

Modes:
- 1 Player: W/S to move; computer controls the right paddle.
- 2 Players: W/S for the left paddle, Up/Down for the right paddle.
- Space: start, pause, resume, and restart after a match.
- First to 7 points wins.

Customization:
Replace PNG files in assets/ with your own images. Keep the filenames and dimensions/aspect ratios compatible.
Every visible game element (background, divider, paddles, ball, score digits, and menu/status text) is drawn from PNG sprite files.
